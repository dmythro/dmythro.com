## What kind of role are you looking for?

Something like a tech lead role, where about 50-70% of the working time is actual development and the rest goes to meetings, planning, mentoring, code reviews and so on. I really enjoy building things myself, and I prefer complex tasks I can focus on without much distraction.

## Are you in Ukraine now? Is it safe?

Yes. I'm at home most of the time, and I'd say it is relatively safe here now. My location turned out to be a pretty good one in this situation, considering I'm quite close to Kyiv (a bit to the south).

## How do you deal with the obvious risks?

I have big batteries that can power the essentials (heating, lights, fiber internet) for a couple of days in winter. I can recharge them from my generators, and switching to batteries and back is automatic and seamless. I have reinforced film on my windows so the glass doesn't shatter (just in case). There is also a safer place below the ground floor. I keep a car with a full tank of gas and some extra, just in case. The main risk is a direct hit, which I hope won't happen.

## Do you plan to leave Ukraine?

No, I have no such plans now. I chose to stay in Ukraine and support my family, people and the army where I can. Of course I have doubts sometimes, so I keep watching the situation from a safety point of view.
