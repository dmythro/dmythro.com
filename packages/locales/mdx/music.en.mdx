Music has always been a big part of my life, and it still is.

Thanks to my father, I was introduced to music in early childhood. I started collecting Pink Floyd when I was about 6 and I'm still a big fan. At 7 I signed up for music school of my own free will, and graduated with honors.

As a teenager I started listening to different metal and electronic music subgenres, and that's when my fascination with music and with playing various instruments went to the next level. Now I play guitars, drums, keyboards and some folk instruments. I have a home recording studio. I'm always curious about sound design and sound quality, instruments, equipment, effects etc.

![Me in my home studio](/me-in-studio.webp)

I still take regular lessons on a few instruments (mainly guitar and drums) to improve my skills.

Since 2011 I've been running an indie record label and music zine in my spare time &mdash; [Noizr](https://noizr.com). It is still a non-profit project: all income is reinvested into developing our artists (since the russian invasion, all the income accumulated over the last couple of years goes to the Ukrainian army and the Come Back Alive foundation).

I also collect some music on physical media (vinyl and cassette tape). Sometimes I set time aside just to listen to my favorite music.
