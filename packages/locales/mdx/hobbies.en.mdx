Music and travel are obviously my biggest hobbies. But I enjoy a lot of other things in this constantly changing world too. I guess I'm just a very curious person.

## Technology & New Gear

I really like getting into new gear and trying it out in the areas I'm involved in. New gear for music (instruments, effects, pedals, audio interfaces and monitors, amplifiers etc.). Smart home devices that make my life easier with automations and sensors. Electric scooters, bicycles etc.

## Active Lifestyle

I really enjoy snowboarding, surfing, swimming, kayaking and cycling. I was a competitive swimmer for years (Candidate for Master of Sport). Some of that doesn't happen very often, but when I start to miss it, I always go back to it. Driving on sand in the desert, huge water parks, roller coasters, a parachute jump &mdash; I've done all of that too.

## Books & Art Books

Reading is not my strong suit. I like some science fiction, Roger Zelazny for example, and Dan Brown, but I don't read that much. On the other hand, I really like art books and collect the interesting ones: about music, art, history, architecture, museums and more.

## Games

There's probably no tech geek who isn't interested in modern gaming. I play some games on my console (mostly online, with friends), sometimes on the iPad. I'm not really into PC gaming anymore: keeping a dedicated gaming PC up to date is too annoying for me, and I don't need one for work. When I'm in the mood to play, I like to just press a button, launch the game and switch off for a while.
